Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026. This explains what Shiya-san stores and why.

What we store

Your Discord user ID and display name, game progress (ChuPoints, rank, stars, inventory, match history, settings you choose such as country), and the IDs of servers the bot is in.

Messages

The bot reads message content only inside cross-server match threads, to pass messages to your opponent. Those chats may be kept briefly so reports can be reviewed. It does not read other messages.

How it’s used

To run games, leaderboards, seasons, reports and moderation. We don’t sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Website

The website’s player search and leaderboards show your game name, rank, stars and game stats to anyone. They never show bans, reports or chat messages. Ask Mitsuka (@youwillknowme_itsjustmichukadara) to hide your profile.

Where it lives

Data is kept in a file on the bot’s host, and only the bot’s admins can access it.

Your choices

Ask Mitsuka (@youwillknowme_itsjustmichukadara) to see or delete your data. Removing the bot from your server stops new data from that server.
