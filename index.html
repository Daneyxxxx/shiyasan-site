How to play

  1. Invite the bot, then type /how. It lists every command. New players start with 40,000 ChuPoints and can claim 10,000 every day.
  2. Open a room. Use /torlclassic or /cupgame to bet ChuPoints (500 minimum). Use /torlranked or /cupgameranked for 7 ranked rounds with no bets. Want opponents from other servers? Try /torlcrossranked or /cupgamecrossranked.
  3. Play your role. Truth or Lie has a Teller and a Guesser. The Cup Game has a Hider and a Guesser. When it’s your turn you have 50 seconds to act.
  4. Earn stars and spend your winnings. Check /profile, /rankleaderboard and /shop.

Rules

  • Play fair. No cheating, scripts or extra accounts.
  • Be kind in cross-server chats. Report problems with /reportmatch within 6 hours of the match.
  • Don’t abuse bugs. Tell Mitsuka instead.
  • ChuPoints are for fun. They have no real-world value and can’t be sold.
  • Idle rooms close after 5 minutes.
  • Admins can restrict or reset accounts that break these rules.
